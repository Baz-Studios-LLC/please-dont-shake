# Changelog

The top section of this file becomes the release notes, and the launcher shows it as
"What's New" — so it's written for whoever is about to play, not for whoever wrote it.

## v0.2.0

Your farm is still there when you come back to it, and it's your hand on the glass.

- **The farm is kept.** Close the game and open it again and the colony is where you left
  it, still digging. Continue walks straight back in. Nothing to save, nothing to load —
  there's one farm and it's the one in the tank.
- **A hand, not a cursor.** Reach out with a fingertip to tap the glass; put your whole
  palm on it and the tank goes wherever your arm does. You can see which one you're about
  to do before you do it.
- **Sand piles like sand.** Poured sand and the spoil the ants carry out now heap into
  cones and find their own angle instead of stacking into spindly towers. Settled sand
  packs down and holds a tunnel; loose sand rolls.
- **A nest with a doorway.** The colony carries its diggings clear of the shaft and drops
  them in a ring, so the entrance comes out as a crater rather than a plug — which is what
  a real one looks like.
- **Settings**, in three tabs. Fullscreen, how loud the piano is, how hard your hand shakes
  the tank, and six skin tones for the hand. Reachable from the title screen and from Esc.
- The studio's mark opens the game, and a press of anything skips it.

## v0.1.0

First light. The farm exists, the sand behaves, and the colony digs its own tunnels.

- A tank of layered sand between glass, sitting in a sunlit bedroom you can see
  through both panes.
- Sand that holds its shape indefinitely when left alone and collapses when shaken.
  Tunnels persist, so the farm accumulates a history — which is the thing shaking
  takes away.
- A founding colony: a queen and ten workers, placed wherever you want them with a
  press-and-hold radial menu.
- Ants that dig for themselves. Nothing about the nest is authored; its shape comes
  out of ants following each other's work.
- Three verbs on one gesture: click to tap the glass, drag to shake the tank, hold
  still to stock it. Tapping alarms the colony without damaging the farm. Shaking
  does both.
- Music, and a sign asking you not to shake it.
